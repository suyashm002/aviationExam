Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Aviation Exam Pro ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by Suyash Mishra. This policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your email address and display name through Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In or email/password).

Exam Data: Your exam scores, answers, and progress are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.

No Personal Data Sold: We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and provide access to the app
  • To store your exam progress and scores locally on your device
  • To display your performance statistics within the app

3. Data Storage

All exam questions, results, and progress data are stored locally on your device using an offline database. We do not upload your exam data to any server.

Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores only your email and authentication tokens. Please refer to Firebase Privacy Policy for details.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication - For user login and account management

5. Data Retention

Your exam data is stored on your device and persists until you uninstall the app or clear app data. Your Firebase account can be deleted at any time by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted authentication through Firebase. All exam data remains on your device and is not transmitted over the internet.

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your account by contacting us
  • Clear all local data by uninstalling the app
  • Request information about what data we store

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
